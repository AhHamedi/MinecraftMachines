Failure → repair timeline

The project’s useful result is a causal chain: each attractive but invalid signal was retained, explained, and converted into a stronger training or evaluation contract.

  1. v1 → v2

    Reward and coordinate shortcuts closed

    Physical progress moved into a fixed spawn frame, post-warmup baselines removed spawn settling, and reset state was synchronized across constrained bodies.

  2. v3 training signal

    A checkpoint appeared to walk reliably

    Generation 6 reported +4.172 / +5.323 / +6.018 blocks across its three training slots with no boundary-sampled failure. That checkpoint is preserved, not promoted.

  3. per-tick audit · rejected

    The apparent success was a sampling false positive

    At speed 0.50, the exact same genome moved +5.595 blocks but fell, escaped its lane, reached body-up −0.0048, and peaked 5.493 vertical blocks. Control-boundary sampling had missed the transient.

  4. v4 diagnosis

    Canonical lane replay exposed a topology confound

    The edge lane failed again while interior lanes passed. Adjacent training floors made interior candidate groups physically different from the benchmark triplet.

  5. v5 benchmark pass · not promotable

    The policy passed the gate; its training provenance did not

    Run 01 passed all eight benchmark criteria with +4.145 mean forward, 0 failures, 0 escapes, 0.659 minimum body-up, and 2.167 peak vertical blocks. A later audit found the grouped arena inserted blank lanes but the training spawner still used contiguous slot offsets. The accepted artifact is preserved as pre-fix evidence, not a final claim.

  6. v6 causal repair

    Arena construction and spawning now share one grouped offset

    The lane-groups-v2 arena remains, the spawner is aligned to its isolated triplets, and the fitness contract is bumped to minecraft_machines:duopod_cem_fitness_v6 so contaminated v5 checkpoints cannot resume as current.

  7. fresh seeded v6 · final gate

    Immutable repeat evidence: ACCEPTED

    A fresh 12-generation search selected generation 5 after the higher-scoring generation 8 failed posture. Both fixed-world, fixed-protocol repeats passed 8/8 with an adjusted margin of +3.0775 blocks.

Evidence-producing architecture

Motion must emerge through the constrained mechanism, while every mutable world or model transition has an explicit recovery or commit boundary.

CEM v6 searchSeed-paired three-speed candidates under the same per-tick stability contract and grouped lane geometry used at publication.
Isolated lane groupsEach candidate gets a benchmark-shaped triplet plus a blank-lane gap; runtime chunk tickets cover the arena.
Physical DuopodBounded left/right servo targets drive three constrained rigid bodies—no position teleport as action.
Per-tick observerPost-warmup progress, minimum body-up, peak vertical excursion, failure, and escape captured every physics tick.
Recovery boundariesAtomic checkpoints heal from backup; a fsynced compressed-NBT arena journal is replayed at startup. A narrow post-restore, pre-chunk-save power-loss window remains documented.
Immutable gateUUID JSON/CSV artifacts, manifest-last commit semantics, shared benchmark ID, SHA-256, and eight explicit criteria.
